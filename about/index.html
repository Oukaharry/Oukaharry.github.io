About

Harrison Odhiambo Ouka, known as Harry Ouka / Ouka Harry

I am a software developer based in Nairobi, Kenya. I build algorithmic trading systems and web applications that support trading workflows.

The public record of that work is my GitHub account, github.com/Oukaharry, created in December 2018. The profile README is the short version of this page: MetaTrader expert advisors and trade-management tools written in MQL4 and MQL5, plus Python and Django applications.

Two repositories carry the product work. TradeManagerEA is a MetaTrader 4 expert advisor. It puts a GUI on the chart for placing trades, setting stop-loss and take-profit levels, and closing part of a position. HedgePass Pro is a Django web application for prop-firm challenge hedging and trade management, with a client dashboard and account settings. A demo of that app is at hedgepass-pro.vercel.app.

I have also completed software engineering coursework in C and Unix/Linux through the ALX program. Those repositories are public: alx-low_level_programming, alx-system_engineering-devops, and CPointers. The earliest ALX repositories, from 2021, describe them as the first ones I published as an ALX student.

I take freelance development work through my MQL5 profiles, _mrharry and _harrison. The direct email is harryodhiambo17@gmail.com. I am also on LinkedIn and DEV.

If you searched for Ouka Harry, Harry Ouka, or Harrison Odhiambo Ouka, you are in the right place. Those are my names. The work on this site is the work in those repositories, and the notes are written from reading them.
